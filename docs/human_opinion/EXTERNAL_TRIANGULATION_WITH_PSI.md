# External Triangulation: Study Φ ↔ Experiment Ψ

How the public-discourse study and the candidate-specific human experiment relate — and the
firewall between them.

## What public discourse (Φ) suggests
Real hiring discourse does **not** support one universal integrity penalty. It shows a
**severity-conditioned, verification-first** policy: ignore trivia, test capability, verify
concerning claims, reject only confirmed material fabrication (`INTEGRITY_DECISION_ATLAS.md`).
For the specific class our detector fires on — technology-tenure anomalies — the engineer/
founder stratum leans **VERIFY/ignore, not auto-reject**. This is *external evidence that Ω's
multi-world utility model is the right shape*: λ is not a single number; the integrity penalty
is a function of severity, intent, evidence, and verifiability.

## What Ψ is designed to measure (and Φ is not)
Ψ measures, under **blinded two-stage conditions on our actual candidates**, the **integrity
reversal rate**: do real reviewers who pick a candidate on career quality (Stage A) *reverse*
once the impossible dates are revealed (Stage B)? That is the candidate-specific causal
quantity — the real λ region (`< 0.10` golden-world vs `≥ 0.10` integrity-world). Φ cannot
produce it: public commenters never evaluate *our* candidates, never under blinding, and never
with the Stage-A→Stage-B causal design.

## What Φ explicitly cannot establish
- It cannot rank or label any Redrob candidate.
- It cannot yield a representative percentage (HN-only, single-coder, n = 19 pilot; platform-
  shaped; upvotes excluded).
- It cannot set a numeric λ — claiming "λ = 0.x" from comments would be false precision.
- It cannot substitute for intercoder reliability or for the blinded Ψ panel.

## How to interpret them together
- **Φ validates the *existence and shape*** of the integrity↔quality trade-off and justifies
  Ω's utility-worlds (World 2 Verification-first and World 3 Severity-conditioned are the ones
  the pilot most supports).
- **Ψ remains the sole *confirmatory* instrument** for the candidate-level decision. The
  pre-registered Ψ shipping rule is unchanged and unaffected by Φ.
- **Firewall:** Φ must never retroactively alter Ψ's questions, thresholds, sampling, or gates.
  If Φ ever motivates a change, it goes into a new `psi_v2/` with its own manifest+hash; the
  original Ψ is preserved.

## Bottom line (the strongest honest claim)
> Public hiring discourse does not support one universal integrity penalty. Reviewers
> distinguish harmless inconsistencies, claims requiring verification, and decisive evidence
> of fabrication — i.e. a severity-conditioned, verification-first decision process rather than
> a binary honeypot flag. Because public discourse does not evaluate our specific candidates
> under blinded conditions, it is used only as external qualitative triangulation; **Ψ remains
> the sole candidate-specific confirmatory instrument**, and golden remains the frozen,
> reproducible submission until Ψ's preregistered gates are satisfied.
