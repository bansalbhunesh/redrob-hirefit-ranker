# Why V6 can win

## Thesis

V6 is not merely the best score we found. It is the strongest combination of
ranking quality, contextual recruiting logic, behavioral-signal integration,
speed, reproducibility, documentation and failure safety.

## 1. It satisfies the actual mission

- Deep JD understanding through compiled role requirements, concept expansion,
  career evidence, seniority and role-family depth.
- Contextual relevance through production/retrieval delivery evidence rather
  than keyword overlap alone.
- Signal integration across profile, career, logistics, activity, availability
  and recruiter-response evidence.
- A 100-candidate ranked, explained shortlist from 100K candidates in 136 seconds.

## 2. It is the strongest all-around measured ranking

| Benchmark view | V6 position |
|---|---:|
| Broad seven-judge mean | #1 / 673 |
| Strongest-union fifteen-judge mean | #1 / 100 |
| Equal four-axis balance | #3 / 322 |
| Main comparison | 30 / 30 composite wins |

Specialists lead isolated slices, but no measured public output dominates V6
across H2, mean7, reviewer and blind simultaneously.

## 3. It is production-shaped

- CPU-only and offline; no hosted per-candidate inference bill.
- Exact input, model, wheel and output hashes.
- Deterministic Python and BLAS environment.
- Atomic publication only after backend, counts, integrity and output checks.
- 3-GiB forced OOM preserves the prior artifact and leaves zero mounted temps.

## 4. It has receipts

- 262 passing tests and 6 environment skips.
- 10,000 / 10,000 corrupted submissions detected.
- 9,750 / 9,750 invalid configurations rejected.
- 883 main/champion fusions evaluated; no free no-loss switch found.
- Multiple learned, semantic and rank-fusion alternatives built and rejected
  when gains did not generalize.

## 5. The positioning is honest

The official challenge page does not publish numeric weights. Our
mission-derived score is **93.7/100**, projected **#1** with an honest **#1–#3**
range. It is explicitly not an official score or leaderboard result. Hidden
labels remain the irreducible uncertainty.

## Judge close

> four-axis system, and the only artifact in our study paired with this level of
> deterministic, hash-pinned, OOM-safe release engineering.
